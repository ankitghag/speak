# Lesson 5.1: High-Frequency Action Verbs for Daily Life & Work

## 1. Lesson Information
- **Day/Topic Number:** 5.1
- **Core Concept:** Mastering the "Power Verbs" that replace weak verb + noun combinations (e.g., "make a decision" → "decide") to sound decisive, natural, and professional.
- **Learning Objective:** Identify and fluently use 15 high-frequency action verbs to replace wordy phrases, instantly increasing speech density and clarity in workplace updates, planning, and daily problem-solving.

---

## 2. Theory & Spoken Patterns

### The "Verb-Noun Trap" vs. The "Power Verb" Shift
In spoken English, we often hide the real action inside a noun (nominalization), forcing us to use a weak "light verb" (*make, do, have, take, give*). This slows down speech and dilutes authority.
*   **Weak:** "I will **make a decision** by Friday." (4 syllables, passive feel)
*   **Strong:** "I’ll **decide** by Friday." (2 syllables, active ownership)

**The Rule:** Find the noun hiding the action → Turn it into the main verb.

### 15 High-Frequency "Power Verbs" (Grouped by Function)

#### A. Planning & Execution (Replacing *do/make/take*)
| Weak Phrase (Avoid) | **Power Verb** | Spoken Formula |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *make a plan* | **plan** | "Let’s **plan** the rollout." |
| *make a decision* | **decide** | "We need to **decide** on a vendor." |
| *take action* | **act** | "We must **act** now." |
| *conduct an analysis* | **analyze** | "I’ll **analyze** the data tonight." |
| *perform a review* | **review** | "Can you **review** this draft?" |
| *make an update* | **update** | "I’ll **update** the tracker." |

#### B. Communication & Influence (Replacing *give/have/make*)
| Weak Phrase (Avoid) | **Power Verb** | Spoken Formula |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *give an explanation* | **explain** | "Let me **explain** the logic." |
| *give a presentation* | **present** | "Sarah will **present** the findings." |
| *have a discussion* | **discuss** | "We should **discuss** the budget." |
| *make a suggestion* | **suggest** | "I **suggest** we pause hiring." |
| *provide guidance* | **guide** | "He’ll **guide** the new hires." |

#### C. Problem Solving & Results (Replacing *solve/fix/achieve*)
| Weak Phrase (Avoid) | **Power Verb** | Spoken Formula |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *find a solution* | **solve** | "We **solved** the bottleneck." |
| *make an improvement* | **improve** | "This **improves** load time." |
| *reach a conclusion* | **conclude** | "We **concluded** the audit." |
| *take responsibility* | **own** | "I **own** that mistake." |

---

### When & Why to Use Them in Real Speech
1.  **Status Updates/Stand-ups:** "I **resolved** the ticket" sounds 10x better than "I *found a resolution for* the ticket."
2.  **Meetings (Agenda Setting):** "We need to **finalize** the scope" drives action; "We need to *make a finalization of* the scope" kills momentum.
3.  **Emails/Slack (Written Speech):** Short verbs scan faster. "Please **approve** this" > "Please *give your approval for* this."

## 3. Natural Spoken Examples & Dialogue

### "How Not to Say It" vs. "How a Clear Speaker Says It"

| # | Context | ❌ Wordy / "Textbook" Style | ✅ Clear Speaker Style | Why the Shift Works |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Stand-up Update** | "I **made an analysis** of the logs and **found a solution** for the error." | "I **analyzed** the logs and **solved** the error." | Cuts 7 words. Puts *you* as the active agent immediately. |
| **2** | **Project Planning** | "We need to **make a decision** on the timeline today." | "We need to **decide** the timeline today." | "Decide" implies closure. "Make a decision" implies process. |
| **3** | **Delegating** | "Can you **do a review** of the contract and **give me your feedback**?" | "Can you **review** the contract and **feedback** me?" *or* "**Review** the contract and **flag** any risks." | "Feedback me" is modern shorthand; "Flag risks" defines the *type* of review expected. |
| **4** | **Owning a Mistake** | "I **take full responsibility** for the delay." | "I **own** the delay." | "Own" is the modern leadership verb. It signals accountability without drama. |
| **5** | **Negotiating/Pushing Back** | "I **have a suggestion**. We should **make a change** to the scope." | "I **suggest** we **scope down**." | "Scope down" is specific industry language. "Suggest" owns the idea. |

### Mini-Dialogue: Sprint Planning (2 People)
**Alex (Lead):** "Okay, we’re over capacity. We need to **prioritize** the login refactor."
**Jamie (Dev):** "Got it. I’ll **scope** that ticket today. Should I **flag** the API dependency?"
**Alex:** "Yes. **Highlight** the risk in the ticket. If it blocks us, we’ll **escalate** to the Platform team."
**Jamie:** "Will do. I’ll **update** you by lunch."
> **Note:** *Prioritize, scope, flag, highlight, escalate, update.* Zero "make/take/have" light verbs.

## 4. Verbal Practice & Content Drills

### Drill 1: The "Verb Swap" (60 Seconds)
**Instructions:** Read the weak sentence aloud. Immediately say the "Power Verb" version. Do not write it—*speak it*. Record yourself if possible.
1.  "We need to **make a plan** for Q3." → "We need to **plan** Q3."
2.  "She **gave a presentation** on the metrics." → "She **presented** the metrics."
3.  "Let’s **have a discussion** about the budget." → "Let’s **discuss** the budget."
4.  "I **took action** immediately." → "I **acted** immediately."
5.  "We **made an improvement** to the UI." → "We **improved** the UI."
6.  "He **provided guidance** to the intern." → "He **guided** the intern."
7.  "We **reached a conclusion**." → "We **concluded**."
8.  "Please **give an explanation**." → "Please **explain**."

---

### Drill 2: 1-Minute Speaking Prompt
**Topic:** *"A Recent Win or Fix"*
**Constraint:** Use **at least 5 different Power Verbs** from the list above (e.g., *analyzed, solved, decided, updated, owned, suggested*). No "make a decision," "do a review," "give an update."
**Script Starter:** *"Last week, I noticed a bottleneck in [process]. I **analyzed** the flow, **identified** the root cause, and **solved** it by [action]. I **updated** the team and **suggested** a permanent fix..."*

---

### Drill 3: Workplace Scenarios (Task → Model Response)

#### Scenario A: The "Blocker" Message (Slack/Teams to Manager)
*   **Task:** You are blocked by a vendor. You need help, but you want to show you’ve done the work.
*   **Model Spoken Response:**
    > *"Hey, flagging a blocker: The vendor **missed** the API deadline. I’ve **escalated** to their lead and **proposed** a workaround using our mock server. I’ll **own** the integration testing once they **deliver**. Will **update** you by 3 PM."*

#### Scenario B: The "Monday Kickoff" (Verbal in Meeting)
*   **Task:** Set the tone for the week. Assign ownership clearly.
*   **Model Spoken Response:**
    > *"Morning team. Three priorities: One, we **finalize** the pricing model—Priya, you **lead** that. Two, we **stress-test** the checkout flow—Mike, you **own** the script. Three, we **document** the release notes. I’ll **review** everything Wednesday. Let’s **execute**."*

## 5. Daily Implementation Action

**The "Light Verb Audit" (Do this once today):**
1.  Open your **Sent Items** email or **Slack/Teams history** from the last 24 hours.
2.  **Find 3 sentences** where you used: *"make a..., take a..., give a..., have a..., do a..., provide a..."*
3.  **Rewrite them mentally (or aloud)** using the single Power Verb.
4.  **Say the new version out loud** 3 times each.

*Example Audit:*
*   *Found:* "I will **make an update** to the deck."
*   *Swapped:* "I’ll **update** the deck."
*   *Drill:* "I’ll update the deck. I’ll update the deck. I’ll update the deck."

**Goal:** Build the "muscle memory" so the Power Verb fires *before* the light verb in live conversation.
